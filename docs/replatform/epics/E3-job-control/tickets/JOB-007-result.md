## Outcome

An operator revocation commits a generation cutoff that DISABLES the target and bumps its `device_generation`. The JOB-004 fence guard's locked current-generation recheck then refuses EVERY governed effect on the old generation as `target_revoked` (renew / event ingest / secret read / artifact commit / completion / projection apply / health / control-ACK) the instant the cutoff commits, BEFORE any per-Organization fanout. A durable operator record drives idempotent, resumable convergence of already-stale leases; it is NOT lease authority.

## Independent check + two concurrency fixes applied

A 2-lane adversarial Workflow. **Lane (frozen-contract & authority integrity): CLEAN** — the `bump` hoist, the new scanner branch, the poll-contract restoration, and the grant keystone all passed. **Lane (revocation correctness): two real concurrency defects the serial tests do not surface:**

1. **HIGH — lost-update / TOCTOU in `revokeExecutionTarget` → silent lost revocation.** The revoke read `device_generation` under `FOR UPDATE` in one transaction (releasing the lock at commit), then disabled the target in a SEPARATE transaction via an exact-generation CAS (`bumpExecutionTargetGeneration ... WHERE device_generation = revokedGeneration`). A concurrent re-enrollment (`advanceTargetGeneration`, which bumps the generation while leaving status active) in the gap made the CAS match 0 rows → silent no-op → the target stayed **active** while `revokeExecutionTarget` returned `revoked:true`; the fanout's re-ensure (same stale generation) also permanently no-oped. A compromised device that re-enrolls during revoke keeps leasing. **FIXED:** the cutoff is now **disable-if-active** (`SET device_generation = device_generation + 1, status='disabled' WHERE id=targetId AND status != 'disabled'`) — idempotent (no-op once disabled) and race-correct (always disables + strictly advances the generation regardless of a concurrent advance; serializes vs `advanceTargetGeneration` on the row lock). Regression test: "the cutoff disables the target even when the live generation advanced past the revoked generation".

2. **MEDIUM — fanout crash-resume gap → job stranded non-terminal.** `convergeOrganization` collected jobs to cancel ONLY from leases it FRESHLY flipped (`status IN offered/active`). A crash between Phase 1 (lease flip committed) and Phase 2 (`requestCancellation`) left the lease `revoked` but the job never cancelled; the resumed tick no longer saw the lease (filtered out), marked the record `completed`, and the job stranded forever (the reaper scans only offered/active leases). **FIXED:** the convergence SELECT now INCLUDES `revoked` leases and re-collects their jobs (no re-flip, no double-release), so a resumed tick re-derives the idempotent cancellation intent from durable lease state. Regression test: "a resumed tick cancels a job whose lease was already revoked before a Phase-2 crash".

The third raised finding (SKIP-LOCKED single-pass convergence timing) was **refuted** — a lease momentarily lock-skipped is still denied by the fence guard and reaped by the reaper backstop; SKIP LOCKED is the intentional choice that avoids the target→lease lock-order inversion.

Both fixes were then re-attacked by a focused second adversarial Workflow (attack-the-fix lanes). **0 confirmed defects** — the one raised finding was refuted: fix 1's invariant holds (the target always ends disabled after a successful revoke; the convergence filter and the frozen `revokedGeneration` are unchanged by the fix), and the disable-vs-converge residual it described collapses into the accepted fence-guard + reaper backstop plus out-of-scope re-placement.

## Known residual (tracked, out of JOB-007 scope)

The durable record freezes `revoked_generation` at the pre-bump read, while the disable-if-active cutoff is generation-agnostic. Under a compound race — a re-enrollment advances the *revoked* target's generation AND a placement+lease is admitted at that intervening generation before the disable commits — the fanout's convergence filter (`leases.target_generation <= record.revoked_generation`) misses that lease, so the fanout does not proactively cancel its job. The security outcome is still correct (the target is disabled; the fence guard denies the stale lease `target_revoked`; the reaper revokes the orphaned lease and recovers capacity). The residual is a *liveness* gap: the reaper's retry is pinned to the disabled target's placement generation, so it is un-leasable and — absent an unplaceable-pending-attempt dead-letter sweeper — the job aggregate may not reach terminal. Clean termination requires re-placement to a live target (**JOB-009**) or such a sweeper; both are pre-existing boundaries, not introduced by JOB-007. Tracked for JOB-009.

Non-goals (deferred): live offer-path capacity enforcement (above), provider adapter/resource, re-homing revoked work beyond the immutable placement policy, the unplaceable-retry dead-letter sweeper (JOB-009).
